# AuraCart - Business Overview & KPI Dictionary

AuraCart is a fictional mid-market ecommerce brand selling consumer electronics, apparel, home living goods, books, and beauty products. To represent a true business environment, the database simulates marketing campaigns, inventory lifecycle management, logistics operations, and web clickstreams.

---

## 👥 Customer Segmentation Model

AuraCart operates on a hybrid B2C/B2B model:
1.  **Regular (B2C)**: Standard customers with lower Average Order Value (AOV) but high volume. Order frequencies are distributed normally, centered around product discovery and coupon codes.
2.  **VIP (B2C)**: High-loyalty customers with high average spend, repeat purchase frequency, and lower sensitivity to price adjustments. They account for the majority of coupon conversions.
3.  **Corporate (B2B)**: Business customers with high order values, large unit volumes, and distinct billing cycles. They typically order bulk batches of office equipment, electronics, and stationery.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Dictionary

These KPIs can be computed directly using the tables and analytical views defined in `schema.sql`:

### 1. Financial KPIs
*   **Gross Sales Revenue**: The raw sum of unit prices multiplied by quantities sold, before any adjustments.
    ```sql
    SUM(quantity * unit_price)
    ```
*   **Discounts / Coupon Burn**: The total value deducted from orders due to active coupons.
    ```sql
    SUM(discount_amount)
    ```
*   **Net Revenue**: The actual sales income generated after discounts are applied (excluding tax and shipping).
    ```sql
    SUM((quantity * unit_price) - discount_amount)
    ```
*   **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: The total manufacturing/supplier cost of all products sold. Historical costs are frozen inside the `order_items` table.
    ```sql
    SUM(quantity * unit_cost)
    ```
*   **Gross Profit**: The net revenue minus the Cost of Goods Sold.
    ```sql
    Net Revenue - COGS
    ```
*   **Profit Margin (%)**: The efficiency percentage representing how much profit is retained for every dollar of sales.
    ```sql
    (Gross Profit / Net Revenue) * 100
    ```

### 2. Customer & Checkout KPIs
*   **Average Order Value (AOV)**: The average amount spent by customers per order transaction.
    ```sql
    SUM(total_amount) / COUNT(order_id)
    ```
*   **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: The total aggregated revenue generated by a single customer across their lifetime.
    ```sql
    SUM(orders.total_amount) GROUP BY customer_id
    ```
*   **Purchase Frequency**: The average number of orders placed per customer in a given timeframe.
*   **Cart Abandonment Rate (%)**: The percentage of created shopping carts that were never checked out.
    ```sql
    (Abandoned Carts / Total Carts Created) * 100
    ```

### 3. Supply Chain & Logistics KPIs
*   **Low Stock Alerts**: Number of items in product inventory where stock falls below the warnings threshold.
    ```sql
    SELECT COUNT(*) FROM product_inventory WHERE quantity_available <= low_stock_threshold;
    ```
*   **Shipping Delay Rate (%)**: The percentage of shipments delivered past their estimated delivery date.
*   **Refund/Return Rate (%)**: The percentage of delivered orders that resulted in full or partial refunds.
    ```sql
    (Refunded Orders / Delivered Orders) * 100
    ```
